An overengineered health and damage awareness mod.



Features:

* Damage vignette: a (short burst) red vignette that appears when you take damage. Stronger hits, stronger vignette. Unaffected by most DoT status effects.
* Note: this mod disables vanilla damage overlay blood texture, for visual consistency.
* Health vignette: a (pulsating, persistent) red vignette that gets stronger closer to death. Starts at 85% by default.
* Heartbeat: a heartbeat sound loop, BPM fluctuates to improve realism. Lower health, faster BPM and louder. BPM spikes with large hits, damage streaks and getting staggered. My attempt at simulating the impact of stress and damage on the heart. Starts at 65% by default.
* has a reasonably extensive config for all the things you'd wanna configure.



Assets used:
