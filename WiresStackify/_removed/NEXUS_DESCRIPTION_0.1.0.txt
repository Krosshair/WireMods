[size=6][b]Wire's Stackify[/b][/size]

Quick stack, restock and sort, all from one little pouch that sits right next to your armor and weight.

[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Krosshair/WireMods/main/WiresStackify/docs/showcase.jpg[/img]

[size=5][b]The pouch[/b][/size]
[list]
[*][b]Left click:[/b] quick stack. Everything you carry goes into nearby chests that already hold the same item.
[*][b]Right click:[/b] restock. Tops your ammo and food back up from nearby chests.
[*][b]Shift + click:[/b] both in one trip, quick stack then restock.
[*][b]Middle click:[/b] sort by type. [b]Shift + middle click:[/b] sort by weight.
[*]With a chest open, the chest gets its own pouch that works with just that chest.
[*]The number on the pouch is how many stacks you're carrying (or how many the chest holds).
[*]Hover it to see how many chests are in range.
[/list]

[size=5][b]Favorites[/b][/size]
[list]
[*][b]Alt + left click[/b] an item to favorite every item of that kind, [b]Alt + right click[/b] a slot to favorite the slot.
[*]Favorites are never quick stacked or sorted, and nothing gets sorted onto a favorited slot. Your hotbar is left alone too.
[*]Coming from Quick Stack Store? Me too! Your favorites come along the first time you load in.
[/list]

[size=5][b]Feels good[/b][/size]
[list]
[*]Every chest you stack into or take from opens with its own sound, one after another, with a little burst of sparks.
[*]The pouch hops, bounces, wiggles or shrugs depending on what just happened, and your items pop into place when sorted.
[*]All of it is only on your side, nobody else hears or sees it.
[/list]

[size=5][b]Compatibility[/b][/size]
[list]
[*]Works with Multi User Chest. Without it, nearby chests are only used in single player, so nobody loses items.
[*]Leaves Equipment and Quick Slots' slots alone.
[*]Doesn't need to be on the server. In multiplayer, sorting a chest someone else is holding needs them to have it too.
[/list]

Quick stacking, restocking and sorting work the way Goldenrevolver's Quick Stack - Store - Sort - Trash - Restock does (MIT licensed), rebuilt with a new interface.
