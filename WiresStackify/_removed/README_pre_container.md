# Wire's Stackify

Quick stack, restock and sort, all from one little pouch that sits right next to your armor and weight.

![Wire's Stackify](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Krosshair/WireMods/main/WiresStackify/docs/showcase.jpg)

## The pouch

* **Left click:** quick stack. Everything you carry goes into nearby chests that already hold the same item.
* **Shift + left click:** restock. Tops your ammo and food back up from nearby chests.
* **Middle click:** sort by type. **Shift + middle click:** sort by weight.
* **Shift + right click:** everything at once. Quick stack, restock, then sort your inventory and every chest it just used.
* **Holding an item?** Click your pouch to quick stack every stack of it, or a chest's pouch to top up your stacks of it from that chest. A pouch opens up when it can do something with it.
* Hover it to see what it would do: stacks a quick stack would take get a yellow ring, stacks a restock would top up get a blue inner ring, and the tooltip counts them.
* With a chest open, the chest gets its own pouch. Same clicks, but only with that chest: quick stack into it, restock from it, sort it. Yours still works with every chest around you, the open one included.
* The number on the pouch is how many stacks you're carrying (or how many the chest holds).
* Every click can be changed in the config.

## Sorting

* Middle click almost anywhere with your inventory open to sort it, no need to aim.
* With a chest open, the top of the screen sorts your inventory and the bottom sorts the chest.

## Favorites

* **Alt + left click** an item to favorite every item of that kind, **Alt + right click** a slot to favorite the slot.
* Favorites are never quick stacked or sorted, and nothing gets sorted onto a favorited slot. Your hotbar is left alone too.
* Coming from Quick Stack Store? Me too! Your favorites come along the first time you load in.

## Feels good

* Every chest you stack into or take from opens with its own sound, one after another, with a burst of sparks (blue ones when restocking).
* The pouch opens up for a moment when it quick stacks or restocks, and hops, bounces, wiggles or shrugs depending on what just happened. Your items pop into place when sorted.
* With Wire's Native Cursor Support, the pointer turns into a hand over the pouch.
* All of it is only on your side, nobody else hears or sees it.

## Compatibility

* Works with Multi User Chest. Without it, nearby chests are only used in single player, so nobody loses items.
* Leaves Equipment and Quick Slots' slots alone.
* Doesn't need to be on the server. In multiplayer, sorting a chest someone else is holding needs them to have it too.

Quick stacking, restocking and sorting work the way Goldenrevolver's Quick Stack - Store - Sort - Trash - Restock does (MIT licensed), rebuilt with a new interface.
