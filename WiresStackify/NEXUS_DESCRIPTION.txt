[size=6][b]Wire's Stackify[/b][/size]

Quick stack, restock and sort, all from one little pouch that sits right next to your armor and weight.

[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Krosshair/WireMods/main/WiresStackify/docs/showcase.jpg[/img]

[size=5][b]The pouch[/b][/size]
[list]
[*][b]Left click:[/b] quick stack. Everything you carry goes into nearby containers that already hold the same item.
[*][b]Shift + left click:[/b] restock. Tops your ammo and food back up from nearby containers, never past what you can carry.
[*][b]Middle click:[/b] sort by type. [b]Shift + middle click:[/b] sort by weight.
[*][b]Shift + right click:[/b] everything at once. Quick stack, restock, then sort your inventory and every container it used.
[*][b]Click while holding an item:[/b] quick stacks every stack of that item. On a container's pouch it tops your stacks of it up from that container instead. A pouch opens up when it can do something with what you're holding.
[*][b]Hover[/b] to see what a click would do: stacks a quick stack would take get a yellow ring, stacks a restock would top up get a blue inner ring, and the tooltip says how many stacks, how many containers and how much weight.
[*]With a container open, it gets its own pouch. Same clicks, but only with that container. Yours still works with every container around you, the open one included.
[*]The number on the pouch is how many stacks you're carrying (or how many the container holds).
[*]Every click can be changed in the config.
[/list]

[size=5][b]Sorting[/b][/size]
[list]
[*]Middle click almost anywhere with your inventory open to sort it, no need to aim.
[*]With a container open, the top of the screen sorts your inventory and the bottom sorts the container.
[/list]

[size=5][b]Favorites[/b][/size]
[list]
[*][b]Alt + left click[/b] an item to favorite every item of that kind, [b]Alt + right click[/b] a slot to favorite the slot.
[*]Favorites are never quick stacked or sorted, not even inside a container, and nothing gets sorted onto a favorited slot. Your hotbar is left alone too.
[*]Items you pick up skip favorited slots unless there's nowhere else to put them (they still stack onto what's already there).
[*]Coming from Quick Stack Store? Me too! Your favorites come along the first time you load in.
[/list]

[size=5][b]Feels good[/b][/size]
[list]
[*]Every container you stack into or take from opens with its own sound, one after another, with a burst of sparks (blue ones when restocking).
[*]The pouch opens up for a moment when it quick stacks or restocks, and hops, bounces, wiggles or shrugs depending on what just happened. Your items pop into place when sorted.
[*]With Wire's Native Cursor Support, the pointer turns into a hand over the pouch.
[*]All of it is only on your side, nobody else hears or sees it.
[/list]

[size=5][b]Compatibility[/b][/size]
[list]
[*]Works with Multi User Chest. Without it, nearby containers are only used in single player, so nobody loses items.
[*]Leaves Equipment and Quick Slots' slots alone.
[*]Doesn't need to be on the server. In multiplayer, sorting a container someone else is holding needs them to have it too.
[/list]

Quick stacking, restocking and sorting work the way Goldenrevolver's Quick Stack - Store - Sort - Trash - Restock does (MIT licensed), rebuilt with a new interface.
