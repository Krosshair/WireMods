[size=5][b]Wire's Upscalers[/b][/size]

NVIDIA® DLSS, AMD FSR™ and Intel® XeSS for Valheim, right in the game's own graphics settings. DLSS works with RenoDX (HDR) and ReShade as a whole, since it's on DX11. Unlike… the other mod that adds upscaling, this one has no dependencies and no additional bloated menus.

In a still screenshot DLAA and Valheim's TAA look very alike. In game DLAA is much sharper: Valheim's TAA is good while nothing moves, and falls apart in motion and on fine detail, which a screenshot doesn't show.

[size=4][b]How it works[/b][/size]
[list]
[*][b]Settings → Graphics:[/b] the upscalers are added to the Smooth/Pixelated row.
[*][b]Resolution:[/b] your game's render scale. At 100% you get the upscaler's native anti-aliasing (DLAA and the like).
[*][b]DLSS model:[/b] Transformer 2 (DLSS 4.5), Transformer (DLSS 4) or CNN (DLSS 3).
[*][b]FSR sharpness:[/b] AMD's own sharpening, off by default.
[/list]
Only the upscalers your PC can run are listed:
[list]
[*][b]NVIDIA® DLSS:[/b] NVIDIA RTX GPUs, DirectX 11 or 12.
[*][b]AMD FSR™ 3:[/b] DirectX 12 only (start Valheim with -force-d3d12).
[*][b]AMD FSR™ 4:[/b] DirectX 12 and an AMD Radeon RX 9000 GPU.
[*][b]Intel® XeSS:[/b] DirectX 12 on any GPU, or DirectX 11 on Intel Arc.
[/list]
FSR and XeSS are untested so far.

[size=4][b]Install[/b][/size]
Put the whole folder in your plugins folder, not just the dll. On first launch the mod copies one file, WiresUpscalersNative.dll, into valheim_Data\Plugins\x86_64: Unity only hands its graphics device to plugins from there. Nothing else outside the mod folder is touched.
Replaces Wire's DLSS: disable or remove it.
