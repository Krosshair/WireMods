Wire's Aldr 0.4.1 (DLL only; manifest/README/CHANGELOG kept from the 0.3.2 package as before)
=======================================================================================

- FIX: Creature biome tiers now come from Valheim's own world spawn lists (ungated spawns; boss-key or event-gated guest spawns only as a fallback). Covers later content automatically. The curated list remains only as a fallback for dungeon/location/quest creatures.
- FIX: Corrected fallback tiers: Hare, Tick, SeekerBrood (Mistlands), Bat (Mountain), Kvastur (Swamp), Lord Reto (Ashlands), Hildir's quest bosses (their own biomes). Removed one unverified entry. Added common variants (piggy, cub, calf, ranged/archer, cave leech, dverger mage variants, etc.).
- FIX: Star roll native-biome check uses the same spawn-data source, so creatures without a hard-coded prefix are no longer silently excluded.
- FIX: Open ocean no longer counts as Meadows for XP. It counts as the Swamp step (serpent waters). Ashlands/Deep North waters already report their own biome.
- NEW (high risk, higher reward): combat/parry XP against a hostile, non-boss creature from a later biome uses that creature's biome for the location multiplier, wherever the fight happens.
- NEW: Fishing XP (reeling and catch completion) uses the later of the current biome and the fish's native biome.
- FIX: Forge of Goodtential jackpots (+2 to +5 quality) now earn upgrade completion XP for every level gained (still capped by SkillReward's 4% per event). Failed/ignored/destroyed attempts still earn nothing.
- VERIFIED: the Deep North progression key Aldr reads exists in the current game data and is the key vanilla uses for that boss's unlocks.
- Build: 0 errors, 0 warnings, against the 15 Oct 2026 assembly_valheim.dll. Not yet tested in game.
- Multiplayer: ServerSync requires the same 0.4.1 DLL on every client and the server.

Foraging rework (same 0.4.1 build, updated 17 Oct 13:1x):
- NEW: Veteran yield. Each Forsaken defeated after a wild resource's own biome boss adds 40% (configurable, "03 - Foraging / Veteran yield per later Forsaken (%)") extra yield per pick, as extra native items. It does not shrink with distance. Example: Black Forest blueberries after Queen = +160%, after Fader = +200%.
- Extra items go through vanilla's own bonus-yield path (same "+N" text and bonus effect, same RPC_Pick count), first pick only, cultivated crops excluded, capped at 12 extra items per pick.
- Unchanged: the existing extra-harvest chance bonus and Swamp thistle / Black Forest mushroom risk premiums.
- Compendium shows each resource's "(extra yield)" with per-boss breakdown.
- New config entry is added automatically with its default; existing settings are untouched.

Materials, raids, Venture (same 0.4.1 build, later on 17 Oct):
- CHANGE: Biome-exclusive materials (copper, tin, iron scrap, ancient bark, silver, obsidian, tar, etc.) keep their own-boss bonus (10%) and gain a flat 25% per Forsaken defeated after their own biome boss (config "04 - Natural Materials / Regional veteran yield per later Forsaken (%)"). At most 4 extra copies per drop. Wood and stone rules are unchanged.
- NEW: Raid defense bonus. +50% combat XP (config "08 - Experience challenge / Raid defense bonus (%)") against vanilla event creatures while you are inside the active raid area. Includes Legacy Raids, since those are vanilla events. Bosses excluded.
- (Venture replaced, see Expedition below.)

Expedition, spoiler-safe Compendium, diagnostics (same 0.4.1 build, later on 17 Oct):
- NEW: Expedition XP (replaces Venture). Up to +25% XP for all skills, config "07 - Experience / Expedition XP bonus cap (%)".
  * Base = vanilla's own player base value >= 3, the same threshold vanilla raids use. Ticks on vanilla's own 2 s base-value refresh (no extra update loop).
  * Builds from horizontal distance travelled off-base (about 5 km to full; jumps over 80 m per tick, e.g. portals, ignored) plus any skill XP earned off-base while you have moved recently (about 150 m in the last minute for full credit).
  * Bonus applies only off-base. At a base it fades over about 7 min (not reset). Standing still off-base for 90 s starts a slow 15 min fade. Death ends it.
  * Compendium: own "Expedition" section after the biome sections. Mod Status is back to exactly the 0.4.0 lines.
- CHANGE: Compendium hides undiscovered things as "???": resource and material names use vanilla's known-materials list, biome headings use known biomes. Material/resource recognition now uses vanilla shared item names verified against game data (fixes two invented later-biome material names).
- Diagnostics (only when "09 - Diagnostics / Enable diagnostics" is on, throttled, no per-frame work): CREATURE_TIERS, EXPEDITION (base enter/leave, every 10% progress, resets), RAID_XP, FORAGE_ROLL now includes veteran extra items, CONFIG lists the new settings.

Expedition (linear) and skill accomplishment rework (same 0.4.1 build, later on 17 Oct):
- CHANGE: "Expedition" now grows linearly, with no fill-up: +2.5% XP per km travelled away from bases (config "07 - Experience / Expedition bonus per km (%)"), plus 20 m worth for each XP event while moving. Cap 200% (config "Expedition bonus cap (%)"). At a base it fades by about 30 percentage points per hour (12 km worth) and doesn't apply; standing still off-base fades at half that after 90 s. Death ends it. Diagnostics log every 5 points.
- NEW accomplishments (share of next level, then normal Aldr XP scaling):
  * Killing blow on a hostile non-boss creature at or above your progression tier: 0.6-1.2% of next level for the weapon skill used (0.3% one tier below, nothing for older foes), x1.5 per star. Recorded on the attacking client, so it also works when another player owns the creature.
  * Sneak attack kill (unaware target, crouching): extra Sneak reward.
  * Felling a whole tree (Woodcutting), scaled by biome and tree hardness.
  * Emptying a deposit that drops a biome-exclusive material (Pickaxes); small multi-area rocks pay per broken area. Works when your game owns the tree/rock, which is the usual case.
  * Harvesting grown crops (Farming).
  * First time obtaining each cooking station or fermenter product (Cooking). Cauldron dishes already count via first crafts.
- REMOVED: combo-position and secondary-attack per-hit bonuses, run/swim/ride travel bonus (Expedition covers travel), per-hit resource hardness bonus, cooking-station recipe-count bonus, jump ledge-landing reward. Kept: counter-after-parry/dodge, moving-target and backstab precision, threat challenge, risky parry, dodge streaks, stealth escape, blood magic shield, fishing, crafting/upgrade rewards.

Player-perspective pass (same 0.4.1 build, 17 Oct afternoon):
- CHANGE: Expedition grows with ACTIVE TIME away from bases: +1% XP per active minute (config "07 - Experience / Expedition bonus per active minute (%)"), plus 0.05% per XP event while on the move. Active = actually travelling (about 40 m in the last minute), or fighting/gathering within 20 s of travelling. A ~30 min expedition is roughly +30-40%. Cap 200%. At a base it fades 1.25% per minute; standing still off-base fades at half that after 90 s; death ends it.
- RESTORED + BETTER: Run/Swim/Ride travel bonus. Up to +40% XP when covering real ground in a steady direction (straight-line progress over the last ~5 XP ticks vs. sprint/swim/ride speed). Circling or running into walls earns nothing.
- RESTORED + BETTER: Combo bonus. Only for consecutive chain steps that actually hit a hostile target: 2nd landed step +12%, 3rd +24%, longer chains up to +36%. A swing that hits nothing breaks the combo.

Simplification (17 Oct, later):
- CHANGE: Expedition "active" = earning any skill XP away from a base (within the last 15 s). No distance or movement checks anymore. +1% per active minute, cap 200%. No XP off-base for 90 s starts a slow fade; at a base it fades 1.25%/min; death ends it.

Balance pass (17 Oct evening):
- Tier: your tier = the biome after your last Forsaken (Bonemass -> Mountain). Creatures are judged against the biome you last conquered: it counts as full value, your unconquered frontier as +1 harder, one older biome reduced, older trivial.
- "% of a level" rewards replaced by flat bonus XP (normal XP gains, scaled by biome/Expedition like everything else):
  kill 2.5 XP same tier, +1 per tier above (max +3), 1 one tier below, none older; +50%/star. Sneak-attack kill +3 Sneak.
  Kills, felled trees and emptied regional deposits are detected on the player's own game (hit within 6 s, target gone, player within 40 m): works in multiplayer, everyone who helped gets it.
  Tree felled 2 + tool tier. Deposit emptied 4 + tool tier. Crop harvest +0.5. First station dish 2. First complex craft 1.5 + 0.5/ingredient type (max 5). Upgrade 1 + 0.5 x new quality per level gained.
  Fish catch 3 + 1.5 per quality above 1 (reel quality bonus back to +25%/level). Risky parry up to ~1.5. Dodge streak 0.05-0.3. Sneak escape 2. Blood magic shield absorb 0.5-2.
- Multi-hit bonus (+3% per extra target) for all weapons. Bows/crossbows: +3% any hit, +8% moving target, + up to 12% by distance; ranged extras cap 25%.
- Fishing reel speed/stamina buffs removed (vanilla reeling).
- Expedition: up to 30 s credited per XP gain; sailing, riding or standing on a ship holds the bonus (no idle fade); status line in the log every 2 min.
- Kills of older creatures are reduced, not removed: 1.25 / 0.6 / 0.3 XP (one/two/three+ older); kill range 120 m for long shots.
- Kill window extends for damage over time and knockback: poison 15 s, fire/spirit 10 s, strong pushes 10 s.
- Blood Magic summons' kills give Blood Magic XP (60% of a kill).
- Stacking: biome and Expedition now ADD (not multiply); all action extras (threat, precision, multi-hit, combo, counter, crafting, sneak) share one pool capped at +60% (+75% ranged).

- Regional ore deposits pay their bonus per chunk broken instead of only when fully mined out (same total).
- Expedition: time spent at base no longer counts toward the clock after leaving.
- Expedition is saved on the character: logging out keeps it, death still resets it.
- Weapon experience transfer: a weapon or magic skill below 75% of your best weapon skill learns up to x2 as fast (config, default 100%), tapering as it catches up. Helps crossbows, magic and switching weapons. Adds to the biome bonus instead of multiplying it, and only boosts the skill's own actions (not kill, barrier or other flat rewards). Does not stack with the low skill catch-up bonus; both now add rather than multiply.
- Blood Magic barriers: 4 XP per baseline (unskilled) barrier worth of hostile damage absorbed, paid per hit, so a stronger barrier is never worse (was 0.5 to 2 XP per hit with a 10 s cooldown).
- Summon kills: a full kill's XP while you fight alongside your summons (attacked, cast or blocked in the last 20 s), otherwise 60% as before. XP your summons earn on their own doesn't advance Expedition.
- Stacking: stars and action extras (threat, combo, precision, travel...) now ADD to the biome bonus instead of multiplying it; flat rewards (kills, trees, deposits, fish, crafts) get half the biome step since they already scale with the target; the high-level threat boost is halved. A 30-minute Ashlands frontier fight went from about 6x to about 4.4x vanilla.
- Weapon transfer starts once your best weapon is 20+.
- Sailing: a ship under way (2+ m/s) builds Expedition at a fifth of the normal pace; it still holds the bonus from fading.
- Cleanup and performance: removed unused jump-landing tracking, recipe familiarity leftovers and diagnostics-only smelter hooks; biome lookup no longer converts to text on every XP gain; creature tiers cached per creature; boss progress read at most once per second; fish spawn checks cached; parry blocker lookup no longer uses reflection per block; building bonus kept separate from crafting.
- Fishing: reeling a higher-quality fish adds +0.25 per quality level instead of multiplying.
- Compendium: Expedition text mentions sailing.
- Ore nodes: each chunk pays base / (2 x sqrt(chunks)), so bigger nodes are worth more in total; mining a node out completely pays the base (4 + tool tier) again as a completion bonus.
- Audit fixes:
  - Expedition only grows while you keep moving (20 m within 6 minutes; portals and sailing count). Camping one spot (AFK running into a wall, trapped-creature farms) no longer builds it; your existing bonus still applies there.
  - The Expedition config value now scales the whole curve, not just the linear part.
  - Creature difficulty is judged by progression only: biomes beyond your frontier now count as +2 and more instead of +1 when you visit them.
  - Area damage (staff explosions, bombs, AoE spells) now counts like any other hit: kill rewards, stars and threat.
  - Perfect dodges work in multiplayer when another player controls the attacker.
  - Kill, tree and deposit rewards no longer miss a target that died just inside its window.
  - Single-object deposits use the same per-chunk and mined-out rewards as multi-chunk ones.
  - Wild onion seeds (Mountains) and wild barley and flax (Plains) now get foraging bonuses, confirmed as Farming pickables in the game data.
  - Removed unused code.
- Blocking: blocks earn more the more dangerous they were: the creature tier bonus (like weapon hits) plus up to +40% depending on how much of your stagger bar the blocked hit filled. Parries keep their larger vanilla XP and the risky-parry reward on top.
- Parries that still stagger you (or empty your stamina) now count as the riskiest parry (full stagger bar) instead of earning no parry bonus.
- Perfect dodges: the vanilla dodge XP now gets the dodged creature as context: stars, its tier, and up to +25% for big creatures (4x your health).
- Cooking: better food gives more Cooking XP (taking dishes off a station and cauldron recipes): +0% for early dishes up to +50% for dishes worth 160+ (health + stamina + eitr, read from the item).
- Riding: a moving mount builds Expedition at full pace, not just at a gallop.
- Sneak: removed the escaped-detection reward (unreliable in multiplayer); sneaking near unaware enemies now gets +35% (was +12%).
- Compendium: new Experience section with the skill XP multiplier where you stand (biome + Forsaken part), your frontier, the weapon learning threshold and what gives bonus XP.

Optimization and fixes (2026-10-18)
- Cooking stations: the food bonus now uses the dish vanilla actually hands you (the first finished slot). Burnt food and putting raw food on get no food bonus.
- Kill and tree/deposit rewards update one entry per target instead of allocating a new one on every hit.
- Creatures not found in any tier list are remembered, so later hits skip the name lookup.
- Blocking without a known attacker no longer touches an outer combat context.
- Forsaken progress is refreshed in place instead of allocating a new array each second.
- Creature tier and dodge caches are cleared on world load.
- Sneak: the +35% now reads vanilla's own 'near unaware enemies' check instead of the XP amount, so XP multipliers from other mods can't trigger it.
- Compendium: Experience and Expedition sections rewritten as data (value in white, source in gray). Adds catch-up threshold, kill XP per known biome, star, extras and raid values. 'Weapon and magic' is now 'Offensive skills'.
