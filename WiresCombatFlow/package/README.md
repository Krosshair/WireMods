# Wire's Combat Flow

Makes Valheim combat feel smoother and more responsive.

Features:

* Cancel attack recovery into a block, dodge, jump, or sprint shortly after your attack hits.
* Each recovery cancel has a configurable extra stamina cost, with an additional cost for secondary attacks.
* Brief input buffering for jump and sprint, plus more forgiving melee combo chaining.
* Gradual movement and turning recovery after attacks, with smoother animation transitions when cancelling.
* Automatic vertical melee aiming towards enemies above or below you, with better collider targeting on slopes. It doesn't change horizontal aim or extend weapon reach.
* Configurable cancel timing, stamina costs, movement recovery, input buffering, and vertical aiming.

Works client-side. Other players don't need the mod.
