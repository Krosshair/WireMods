[size=6][b]Wire's Camera Tweaks[/b][/size]

[b]A camera that feels like a lens. Depth of field that blurs like a real one, shake that hits without wobbling, and zoom that glides.[/b]

In short:

[list]
[*][b]Depth of field like a camera.[/b] Look at something close and what's behind it softens into round bokeh. Look into the distance and nothing blurs.
[*][b]It never gets in your way.[/b] Focus follows what you look at and ignores your own character.
[*][b]Shake with weight.[/b] Short hits kick, big events settle into a low rumble instead of a wobble.
[*][b]Smooth zoom.[/b] The mouse wheel glides, and the camera eases back out after a wall pushed it in.
[/list]

Focus on the table: what's right at the lens melts into the foreground, the room behind it softens, and the bright spots turn into bokeh.

[size=5][b]Features[/b][/size]

[size=4][b]Depth of field[/b][/size]

Needs Valheim's Depth of Field graphics setting on. Two qualities:

[list]
[*][b]Vanilla:[/b] Valheim's blur with smoother focus. Focus moves the same at any frame rate instead of snapping at high FPS and dragging at low.
[*][b]Lens Blur (default):[/b] the way games like Call of Duty do bokeh. Blurred foreground spreads softly over what's behind it, the background never bleeds over something sharp, and bright highlights become round discs.
[/list]

With Lens Blur:

[list]
[*][b]Focus like a real lens:[/b] how blurry something is depends on how much closer or further it is than what you look at, so far views stay crisp and close-ups get depth.
[*][b]Focus from an area,[/b] not a single point, so a branch crossing the centre of the screen doesn't make it jump. Your character is never the focus and never blurs.
[*][b]Near blur[/b] for grass or a branch between the camera and your character.
[/list]

[size=4][b]Camera shake[/b][/size]

[list]
[*][b]Weaker overall.[/b] Short impacts keep a punchy kick, bigger events turn into a lower, sustained rumble with subtle secondary motion.
[*][b]Directional impacts:[/b] hits push the camera the way they came from.
[/list]

[size=4][b]Smooth zoom[/b][/size]

[list]
[*][b]Mouse wheel and controller zoom glide[/b] to where you asked.
[*][b]After a wall pushes the camera in,[/b] it eases back out instead of snapping.
[/list]

[size=5][b]Settings[/b][/size]

[size=4][b]3 - Depth of Field[/b][/size]

[list]
[*][b]Quality:[/b] Vanilla or LensBlur.
[*][b]Aperture:[/b] lens opening in millimetres for Lens Blur. 30 is about a 50 mm f/1.8 photo lens. Higher blurs more.
[*][b]Blur In Time / Clear Time:[/b] how fast focus follows what you look at.
[*][b]Near Blur:[/b] blur things between the camera and your character.
[/list]

Shake and zoom strengths are under [b]1 - Shake[/b] and [b]2 - Smooth Zoom[/b].

[size=5][b]Performance[/b][/size]

Lens Blur runs on the graphics card and is barely measurable on an RTX 4090. On the processor it costs less than Valheim's focus check, which it replaces.

[size=5][b]Compatibility[/b][/size]

Works with RenoDX: its HDR highlights make the bokeh brighter.
