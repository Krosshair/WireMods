# Wire's Camera Tweaks

1. Smoothens out camera zooming.
2. Improves Valheim's camera shake. Overall weaker shake. Short impacts retain a punchy kick. Larger events transition into a lower-amplitude sustained rumble with subtle secondary motion.
3. Smoother Depth of Field focus, plus an optional lens-like blur: look at something close and the background softens, look far and nothing blurs. Slight near blur at the lens.
